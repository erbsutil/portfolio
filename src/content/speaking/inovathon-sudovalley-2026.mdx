Mentored teams during the two-day Inovathon Sudovalley at ACEFB in Francisco Beltrão, part of Mundo Tech at Expobel 2026. Nine teams reached final proposals for fair-related challenges.

My focus was practical: help teams use AI tools to prototype faster, tighten the pitch, and cut scope when the idea was too wide. Less pep talk, more “what ships by Sunday.”

Top three projects were rewarded for impact against the event brief.
