Presented at DevParaná in Pato Branco. Focus: React bundle cost vs Astro Islands, how selective hydration works, and the Mega Island mistake: wrapping the whole page in `client:load` and wiping the win. Included Next.js vs Astro build-output comparisons and the size difference with islands vs a full-page island.
